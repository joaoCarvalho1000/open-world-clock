Your cities, always in view.

Keep other time zones beside your work. A free Windows clock you can pin above your other windows.

For Windows

  1. Install Open World Clock from Microsoft Store.
  2. Add the cities you follow, then select the pin in the top bar.
  3. Choose a strip, compact bar or vertical column. Unpin it whenever you want.

Keep it on your Windows desktop

The browser tool is handy for a quick check. Staying above other apps is a feature of the Windows desktop app.
