Support

I make Open World Clock on my own, and I read every message. Stuck, found a bug, or missing something? Write to me.

Email me Report a bug or suggest an idea
  • Version 1.3.2
  • Windows 10 and 11
  • The iPhone app is in development.

Email me

Write to support@openworldclock.com. It’s the same address as on the privacy policy, and it comes straight to me.

For a problem, three things help me answer in one go: the version (in Settings, next to the name, for example v1.3.2), your Windows version, and where you got the app: the Microsoft Store, the installer or the portable exe. For anything you can see, a screenshot says more than a paragraph.

Report a bug or suggest an idea

Bugs and ideas work best as issues on GitHub, where other people can find them, add what they know and follow along. Open an issue on GitHub. You need a free GitHub account for that; if you don’t have one, email works just as well.
