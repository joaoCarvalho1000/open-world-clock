London to New York time difference

New York is usually 5 hours behind London (EST/EDT vs GMT/BST). When both cities are on standard time or both on daylight saving, 14:00 in London is 09:00 in New York. For a few weeks each spring and autumn the gap is 4 hours because the US and UK change clocks on different dates.

That is why a date picker matters. “London time vs New York” is not one fixed offset for every day of the year. Use the converter above for a specific date, or check the seasonal table below.

Shared working hours (London ↔ New York)

For a typical 09:00-17:00 office day in both cities on a 5-hour gap, the useful overlap is roughly 14:00-17:00 London / 09:00-12:00 New York. Night-shift or flexible teams get more room. Open the meeting planner with London and New York preloaded to paint working hours, including overnight shifts.

FAQ

How many hours between London and New York?

Usually five. For short windows around the US and UK daylight-saving changes, the difference is four hours. Always confirm with a date.

What is 2pm UK time in New York?

On a normal 5-hour day, 14:00 in London is 09:00 in New York. Near DST transitions it can be 10:00 - use the converter above.

What is 8pm New York time in London?

On a normal 5-hour day, 20:00 in New York is 01:00 the next calendar day in London.

Is New York ahead of or behind London?

New York is behind London. Morning in London is still night or early morning on the US East Coast.

Can I keep both clocks on my Windows desktop?

Yes. Open World Clock pins city cards above other windows, works offline, and has no ads or telemetry. Free for Windows 10 and 11.
