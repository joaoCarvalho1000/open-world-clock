## Description (under 10,000 chars)

Open World Clock keeps the cities you care about on your Windows 10 or 11 desktop, pinned above your other windows if you like, and tells you in one look whether it is morning, midday or the middle of the night there. It costs nothing and asks for nothing in return: no ads, no account, no telemetry, no upsell. You should not have to pay for good software like this.

WHO'S AWAKE, AT A GLANCE
Every city gets its own card with a large clock, the date, the UTC offset and the difference from your local time. Cards follow each city's real sunrise and sunset, calculated on your PC, so they turn to night at the actual sunset instead of at a fixed hour, with sky colors from dawn to dusk. Hover a card to see today's sunrise and sunset times.

CONVERT ANY TIME
Type a time like 3pm, 9:30 or 15h30, pick the city it belongs to, and every clock shows the matching time. Cards mark +1 day or -1 day when the date changes, and cities where people are likely asleep (22:00 to 07:00) dim and show a moon. Prefer the mouse? Scroll over any card, or drag along its day line, to move every clock in 15-minute steps. Pick another day with the date shortcuts, and press "Back to now" when you are done.

PLAN MEETINGS ACROSS TIME ZONES
The meeting planner stacks a 24-hour row for each city, shades working hours and nights, and highlights the hours when everyone is at work, with a summary like "3 h overlap". When no hour works for everyone, it shows the hours when the most cities are at work. Set working hours and weekend days per city, including night shifts, and jump between today, tomorrow and the rest of the week.

THE WORLD MAP
Open the world map to see day and night as it happens, where the sun is right now, and every city on your list.

MADE FOR YOUR DESK
- Strip, compact or vertical layout, each remembering its own size.
- Pin it above all windows, or let it sit with the rest.
- Windows 11 Snap and a native acrylic backdrop.
- Light, dark or system theme, with adjustable background opacity.
- Custom labels: show America/Los_Angeles as "San Francisco", or name a card after a teammate.
- Copy all converted times with one click and paste them into a chat or an invite.
- Clock-change notes before daylight saving time starts or ends.
- 12 or 24-hour clock, optional seconds.
- Search by city, country, abbreviation (EST, CET) or offset (UTC-5, +3).
- Lives in the tray: closing the window keeps it running, it remembers its position, and it can start with Windows (Settings > Apps > Startup).
- English, Portuguese and Spanish.
- Works with the keyboard, screen readers and Windows contrast themes, and zooms from 100% to 200%.

FREE, OPEN SOURCE, NO TELEMETRY
Good software should be good and free. Open World Clock has no price, no in-app purchases, no ads, no account and no upsell. It collects no data and has no telemetry: the app does not connect to the internet, time zone rules are built in, and everything works offline. Your cities and preferences stay in a settings file on your PC. The source code is open under the MIT License, so anyone can read it. Software like it should be.
