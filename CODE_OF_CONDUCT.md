# Code of conduct

Open World Clock is a small project, and I want everyone who takes part in it, in issues, discussions or anywhere else it's discussed, to have a good experience.

## Expected

- Be kind and patient, including with people who are new or whose first language isn't English.
- Keep feedback about the work, not the person.
- Assume good intent, and ask when something is unclear.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory jokes and language.
- Personal attacks, trolling, or deliberately derailing a conversation.
- Publishing anyone's private information without their permission.
- Sexual language or imagery, or unwelcome attention of any kind.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, issues and other contributions that break these rules, and may block people who repeatedly do.

To report a problem, email support@openworldclock.com. Reports are read only by the maintainer and kept confidential.
