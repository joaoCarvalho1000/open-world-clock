## Description

The best world clock for iPhone. Completely free, no strings attached.

Open World Clock shows the time in every city you care about, on cards colored by the sky in each city: light blue by day, navy at night, warm tones at dawn and sunset.

Convert a time
Tap a city and drag the wheel: every clock moves with it. Type a time if you already know it, pick a day from the date chips or any date from the calendar, then tap Now to go back. Cards show when a city is a day ahead or behind, and which cities are likely asleep. Copy times puts the converted times on the clipboard, ready to paste into a message or an invite.

Plan a meeting
The Planner lines up the hours of all your cities on one day and marks the hours when everyone is working. Set the working hours and working days of each city, overnight shifts included. When no hour works for everyone, it shows the best one and how many cities are working then.

See day and night
The Map shows where it is day and night right now, with the sun and a dot for each of your cities. Touch and hold a city for its sunrise and sunset times. Cards also tell you when the clocks are about to change for daylight saving time.

Widgets
Small, medium and large widgets for the Home Screen, circular, inline and rectangular widgets for the Lock Screen, and big digits in StandBy. Tap +1 h on the medium or large widget to see every city an hour ahead. It goes back to the current time on its own after an hour.

For everyone
12 or 24 hour clock, light and dark themes, English, Portuguese and Spanish. VoiceOver reads each city with its time and how far ahead or behind it is, and the layout adapts to the largest text sizes.

Free and open source
You should not have to pay for good software like this. No ads, no account, no in-app purchases, no upsell. The code is open source under the MIT License, so anyone can read how the app works.

No data collected
No analytics and no tracking. The app works offline and your cities stay on your iPhone.

Software like it should be.
