## Maker comment

Hi, I'm João. I wanted a world clock on Windows that was actually good and couldn't find one, so I'm building it and
giving it away.

Open World Clock keeps the cities you care about on your desktop. Each card turns light or dark with that city's
real sunrise and sunset, so you can tell at a glance who is awake. Type a time or scroll the mouse wheel over a card
to convert it across every city, and the meeting planner shows the hours your team overlaps, or the hours when the
most cities are at work when no hour works for everyone.

It's completely free, with no strings attached. I don't think you should have to pay for good software like this.
No ads, no account, no telemetry, no data collection, no upsell. It works offline, it's in English, Portuguese and
Spanish, and the code is open source under the MIT license, so anyone can read it. Software like it should be.

You can try it in your browser first, with nothing to install: the web version is the same app, built from the same
files. An iPhone version is in progress.

I'd love to hear what's missing for how you work across time zones.

Website, where you can also try it in your browser right on the home page: https://openworldclock.com/?utm_source=ph&utm_medium=social&utm_campaign=launch-1.3.0
